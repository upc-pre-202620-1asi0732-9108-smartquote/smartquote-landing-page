    Where the platform sits in your process

    1. The farm registers what it needs

      Production and animal health state the item, quantity, required date and technical conditions from the mobile app. Those conditions become the criteria everything else is measured against.

    2. You drop in the quotations

      PDFs, however they arrived. Damaged or duplicated files are flagged one by one, without stopping the rest of the batch.

    3. The analysis reads and normalises

      Prices per sack become prices per tonne. Every field comes back with a confidence level and a link to the exact line it was read from. What it cannot determine, it leaves empty.

    4. You verify, then simulate

      Correct anything the analysis got wrong — the original value, your correction and your reason are all kept. Then run the comparison you just tried above.

    5. The order comes out linked

      Generated from the awarded quotation and tied to the simulation behind it. If a criterion changes afterwards, the order is blocked until you run it again.

    Six months later, the answer is still there

    Select any link in the chain. Everything before it stays reachable, which is what turns a decision into something you can defend in an audit.

    Questions we get asked

    What happens if the analysis reads a value wrong?

    You correct it before the quotation enters a simulation. The platform keeps the original value, the corrected one, who changed it, when and why.

    Our suppliers send scanned quotations. Does that work?

    A scan is read like any other PDF, but confidence levels tend to be lower and more fields come back for review. The platform tells you which ones instead of guessing.

    Does SmartQuote decide for us?

    No. It structures the information and shows the reasoning behind a ranking. Approving a supplier and issuing the order stays with your procurement team.

    What if we want the cheaper quotation anyway?

    You can award it. The platform records that it failed a mandatory criterion and the reason you gave, so the exception is documented instead of invisible.

    Does it replace our ERP?

    No. SmartQuote covers the stretch between the request and the purchase order, which is where the comparison happens. The order it produces is meant to continue in your existing system.

    Where does supplier information end up?

    Documents and extracted data stay inside your account. Access is limited to the users you register, and every correction and approval is attributed to a person.
